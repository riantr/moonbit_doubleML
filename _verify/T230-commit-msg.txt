Release 0.23.0: GainStatsSource::from_blp_hc0 (HC0-honest nu2)

Added
- sensitivity.mbt::GainStatsSource::from_blp_hc0
  (blp, n_folds?, seed?): HC0-honest variant of
  from_blp_cv. Same cross-fit var_y_residuals as
  from_blp_cv, but a different nu2 formula:
  nu2[k] = (1 / n_obs) * ||M[k,:] @ basis^T||^2
  where M = (basis^T basis + ridge I)^{-1} is the
  BLP's regression matrix. This is consistent with
  the upstream
  doubleml.utils._estimation._compute_sensitivity_elements
  convention, where nu2 = E[score_d^2] and the
  score is the per-observation influence on the
  k-th coefficient.

Why a separate function
- The homoskedastic OLS formula
  nu2 = var_y_residuals / (n_obs * se^2) is only
  correct under homoskedasticity (where
  se^2 = sigma^2 * (Z^T Z)^{-1}_{kk}).
- The HC0 SE
  se^2 = sum_i (M[k,:] @ x_i)^2 * e_i^2
  does not satisfy the same relation.
- The projection-weight formula is the
  HC0-compatible alternative.
- Under homoskedasticity the two formulas agree
  exactly; under heteroskedasticity they differ
  in a way that captures the per-observation
  "weight" the basis has on the k-th coefficient.

Notes
- The intercept nu2[0] is set to 1.0 (sentinel),
  matching the convention from from_blp and
  from_blp_cv. The intercept doesn't have a
  "projection weight" in the OLS sense.
- coef, se, var_y, all_coef are unchanged from
  from_blp / from_blp_cv.
- The regression matrix M is recomputed inside
  from_blp_hc0 (the LinearRegression learner only
  stores the diagonal of M, not the full matrix,
  so we re-invert to get the full M). This is a
  one-time cost per from_blp_hc0 call and is
  negligible for the typical BLP dimensions
  (p <= 10).
