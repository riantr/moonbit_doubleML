docs: add REVIEW-0.4.3 (1 high / 1 medium / 5 low)

A read-only review of the 0.4.3 codebase. Findings:

High
- H2: DoubleMLAPO::fit inlines the var_est calculation (apo.mbt:163-176)
  instead of using the shared helper. 13 lines of duplication that risk
  drift if var_est is upgraded (e.g. Kahan compensation extension).

Medium
- M13: kfold.mbt carries its own 7-bit seed encoder (kfold.mbt:33-49)
  while seed_to_bytes is the canonical 8-bit encoder. Same integer seed
  produces different fold partitions depending on which encoder is used.

Low (5)
- L10: README test count is stale (113 → 115 after 0.4.1 + 0.4.3).
- L11: DoubleMLLPQ::fit inlines var_est-style variance (lpq.mbt:220-224).
- L12: LogisticRegression::fit does not validate y in {0, 1}.
- L9: stale comment in kfold.mbt folded into M13.
- L13: confirmation that the 4 deferred items (M5/L1/L3/L4) remain
  deferred with reason.

VERDICT: PASS (release-ready). H2 + M13 should be fixed in 0.5.0.
