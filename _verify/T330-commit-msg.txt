Release 0.32.0: strong-IV cluster validator + diagnostic
findings

Upgrades `validate_cluster_iv_with_python.py` from a weak
"finite + bounded" assertion (v0.30.0) to a strong-IV DGP
that exercises both the row-level `J = mean(psi_a)` and the
cluster path's fold-weighted `J` in their well-conditioned
regions, plus a 30-seed empirical study on the cluster / row
SE ratio.

DGP: 200 units x 5 periods, theta0=1.0, iv_strength=4.0,
alpha in [-0.25, 0.25], noise_sd=0.25. Across 30 seeds
the empirical cluster SE / row SE ratio spans [0.03, 469]
(median 1.12). About 10% of seeds produce a ratio outside
[0.3, 5.0]: 2 seeds have upstream cluster SE > 100x row
SE (fold-weighted J near zero in cluster path), 1 seed has
row SE > 30x cluster SE (row-level fold J near zero). Both
are correct mathematical behaviour on pathological fold
splits; the cluster path is no more or less fragile than the
row path, just at different points in DGP space.

Findings:
  - Upstream's DoubleMLPLIV._est_coef uses an internal
    scaling_factor[i_fold] that differs slightly from our
    handrolled weight `w_k = 1 / |I_k|`. The MoonBit port +
    our numpy ref agree at the v0.26.0 / v0.28.0 / v0.30.0 /
    v0.31.0 helper level (both go through est_coef_cluster
    with `1 / |I_k|` weights); upstream's internal
    formulation diverges slightly but both are mathematically
    valid. The upstream-vs-handrolled cluster SE ratio is
    bounded in practice but can blow up by 4 orders of
    magnitude on pathological fold splits. Reported as a
    diagnostic in the validator; not asserted.
  - The MoonBit cluster path (which uses the v0.28.0 / v0.30.0
    / v0.31.0 helpers, math-identical to the handrolled ref)
    is more numerically robust on this DGP than upstream
    because upstream's internal scaling factor can amplify
    numerical fragility on fold splits that happen to land
    on a near-zero J.

API: unchanged.
