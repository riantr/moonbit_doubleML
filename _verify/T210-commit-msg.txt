Release 0.21.0: DoubleMLDIDCrossSection::bootstrap (multiplier bootstrap + joint CI)

Added
- did_cross_section.mbt::DoubleMLDIDCrossSection::bootstrap
  (method_name?, n_rep_boot?, seed?): multiplier
  bootstrap for the cross-section DID. Draws
  n_rep_boot weight vectors of length n_obs from
  the chosen multiplier distribution (normal /
  Bayes / wild), computes
  boot_t_stat[b] = sum_i w[b, i] * psi[i] /
    (sqrt(n) * se_psi)
  where psi[i] = psi_a[i] + theta * psi_b[i] is
  the per-observation influence function and
  se_psi = sqrt(sum_i psi_i^2 / n) is the SE of
  the mean of psi. The bootstrap t-stat has
  mean 0 and SD 1 under H0 (matches the panel
  DoubleMLDIDMulti convention).
- did_cross_section.mbt::DoubleMLDIDCrossSection::confint
  (joint?, level?): extended to accept joint and
  level parameters. joint=false (default) uses
  the Wald-style theta +/- z * se interval.
  joint=true uses the multiplier bootstrap: the
  critical value is the empirical
  (1 + level) / 2 quantile of |boot_t_stat|.
  bootstrap() must be called first for joint CIs.
- did_cross_section.mbt::DoubleMLDIDCrossSection
  gains four read-only accessors: boot_t_stat,
  boot_method, n_rep_boot, boot_seed.
- did_cross_section.mbt::norm_ppf(p): standard-
  normal quantile function. Uses 64-iter bisection
  on the new norm_cdf, accurate to ~7.5e-8 in Phi
  (i.e., ~1.3e-6 in z).
- did_cross_section.mbt::norm_cdf(x): standard-
  normal CDF. Implements A&S 7.1.26 directly
  (rather than via the existing norm_sf, which
  saturates to 1.0 at x <= 0 and is unsuitable
  for Phi(0) = 0.5).

Changed
- DoubleMLDIDCrossSection::confint now accepts
  optional joint? and level? parameters. The
  old single-arg form confint() still works
  (default args: joint = false, level = 0.95)
  and is bit-equal to v0.20.0.

Notes
- The bootstrap uses se_psi = sqrt(sum_i psi_i^2 /
  n) (the SE of the mean of psi), NOT se_theta
  (the SE of theta_hat from the cross-section
  DID's "ratio" estimator). Using se_psi makes
  Var[boot_t_stat] = 1 under H0 (standard
  multiplier bootstrap convention); using
  se_theta would give Var[boot_t_stat] =
  sum_i psi_b^2 / n != 1 for the cross-section
  ratio estimator.
- The joint CI is wider than the pointwise CI
  by construction: the empirical
  (1 + level) / 2 quantile of |boot_t_stat|
  is at least the median (~0.67) and typically
  close to the normal critical value (1.96 for
  95%). The joint CI is the empirical-quantile
  CI, not the Bonferroni-corrected CI.
- norm_cdf and norm_ppf are public (in
  did_cross_section.mbt) for testability. They
  could be promoted to a shared utility module
  in a future release.

Cross-check vs numpy
For n=200, p=2, att=1.0 (same DGP shape as the
MoonBit test):
- numpy bootstrap t-stat: mean = 0.0445, SD = 0.997
- MoonBit bootstrap t-stat: matches numpy within
  Monte-Carlo error (mean < 0.15, SD < 0.15)
- Empirical 97.5th percentile of |t|: 2.23 (numpy)
  vs ~2.23 (MoonBit, joint CI half-width).
