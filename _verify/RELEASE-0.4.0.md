## What's NOT in this release

* HC0 sandwich SE for RDD (only the WLS-homoskedastic form is implemented).
* Multi-level PolicyTree recursion beyond the depth-N grows to leaves (no
  tree-pruning or `min_samples_leaf`).
* `LogisticRegression` is the only classifier; upstream non-tree classifiers
  (RandomForest, etc.) are not ported.
* Hyperparameter tuning, cluster-robust SE, sensitivity analysis, IPW-
  normalized scores are not included.
