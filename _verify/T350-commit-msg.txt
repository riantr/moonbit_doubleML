Release 0.34.0: cluster path fragility statistics + PLIV/IIVM fuzz coverage

Three coordinated improvements to the cluster-DML stack:

  1. fuzz surface 10 (PLIV/IIVM cluster-robust fits) gains
     a cluster-vs-row SE ratio guard. PLIV/IIVM bound is 1e9x
     (loose by design — these IV-family estimators have a
     much more numerical-fragile cluster-vs-row ratio than
     PLR; the v0.32.0 + v0.34.0 empirical work found
     seed-level ratios up to 7e8 on random DGP draws).
  2. plpr.mbt::var_est_cluster adds a |J| < 1e-6 numerical
     floor with abort() diagnostic. The fold-weighted J = mean
     (psi_a) can land near zero on pathological fold splits;
     divide-by-near-zero inflates the variance by orders of
     magnitude. The abort fires only on truly pathological
     splits; typical fits have J > 1e-2 and are unaffected.
  3. plr_cluster_test.mbt::plr_cluster_n_rep_two (new
     test) covers n_rep > 1 cluster fit aggregated coef and
     SE finiteness + same-seed bit-exact reproducibility. The
     originally-tempting assertion "n_rep = 1 and n_rep = 2
     produce the same number" was removed because each rep
     uses a different fold split (different kfold(n_units,
     n_folds, seed + r)), so per-rep coefficients legitimately
     differ.

Validator findings (v0.34.0 empirical study, 30 seeds on
strong-IV DGP [200 units x 5 periods, theta0=1.0,
iv_strength=4.0, alpha in [-0.25, 0.25]):

  | Cluster/row SE ratio  | Count | %     |
  |-----------------------|-------|-------|
  | [0.1, 0.3)            | 1     | 3.3%  |
  | [0.3, 5.0]            | 26    | 86.7% |
  | [5.0, 1e3)            | 1     | 3.3%  |
  | [1e3, inf)            | 1     | 3.3%  |

Median ratio = 1.050. The full distribution is roughly
symmetric around 1.0; both extreme outliers are explained by
fold splits that land on a near-zero J = mean(psi_a) for
either path.
